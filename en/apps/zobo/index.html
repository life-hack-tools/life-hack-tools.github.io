About

It doesn't list everything.
Only what's different today.

Nobody forgets their keys, wallet, or phone. What you forget is the thing you don't usually carry, on the day that isn't usual. Zobo is a checklist for before you head out. It tells you, in one line, only what belongs to that day, such as your passport, patient card, stove, and door lock. There is no account and there are no ads, and your data stays on your device.

* The mascot is Zobo, a baby elephant, because an elephant never forgets.

How it works

Three steps, calmly.

1

Create a plan

Choose Flight, Hospital, Trip, or Plan, then add a name, a date, and a departure time. Items that belong only to that day are added for you, based on the type.

2

See only what matters now

Tasks fall into "this week", "day before", "morning of", and "at the airport". You only see what matters now, and the rest can wait.

3

Check and head out

On the day, check things off one by one. Each check is saved with a time, so if you start to wonder "did I...?" after leaving, one look settles it.

Features

No nagging, by design.

Only what's different today

Everyday items stay off the list. Only what belongs to that day is shown, so the list stays short and you keep using it.

Split by time

Tasks are split into "this week", "day before", "morning of", and "at the airport". For international flights, passport validity and online check-in are added for you.

Set it up the night before

Add tomorrow morning's items tonight. In the morning, just look.

A record you can trust

Every check is saved with a timestamp. If you start wondering "did I...?" on the way, one look settles it.

Gentle reminders

At most two reminders per plan, at 8 pm the day before and 7 am on the day. No counters pressuring you, and nothing on days with no plans.

Your data stays on your device

No account and no ads. Your plans and checks are stored on your device and are not sent anywhere. Available in English, 日本語, and Tiếng Việt.
