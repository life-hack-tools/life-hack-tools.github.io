About

No need to rush.
You'll still make the bus.

Ruga lets you check bus departure times and your walking time to the stop together. It combines the time to the stop from where you are, a safety margin, and the next departures, and recommends only the buses you can catch if you leave now. It does not recommend buses you would have to rush for. The first service area is Keio Bus around Hashimoto Station (between Hashimoto Station and Minami-osawa Station).

Timetables are based on Keio Bus data from the Public Transportation Open Data Center (ODPT). Accuracy and completeness are not guaranteed.

How it works

Three calm steps.

1

Choose a stop

Pick a nearby stop based on where you are, or choose one yourself without using location.

2

See how many minutes are left

The next bus, the ones after it, and your walking time to the stop, all at a glance.

3

Leave with room to spare

Head out on a bus that works if you leave now. Ruga values having a little time to wait at the stop.

Features

Not rushing, made normal.

Next departures

Shows the next bus and the ones that follow.

Easy walking time

Works out the walk from where you are to the stop, on your device.

Recommends with margin

Adds a safety margin and recommends only buses you can catch without rushing.

Set your walking pace

Choose slow, normal or brisk, whichever suits you.

Location stays on your device

Your location is never sent to a server. You can still choose a stop yourself without allowing it.

Clear about its sources

Shows where the timetable comes from and when it was fetched. With no connection, it marks the data as older and makes no recommendation.
