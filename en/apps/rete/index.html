About

paper stamp cards.

Rete is a digital stamp and rewards app for restaurants and cafes. Scan a QR code to collect stamps and redeem rewards. Simple for both shops and customers.

Features

Simply connected.

QR Stamps

Scan the shop's QR code. Collect stamps with a single tap.

Redeem Rewards

Exchange stamps for rewards. Shops set their own offers.

Find Shops

Discover participating shops nearby on the map.

Shop Dashboard

Shops manage stamps, rewards, and visit analytics in one place.
