About

Never lose your car in
a parking lot again.

Pitto is a parking-memo & navigation app that helps you compare places to park near your destination, save where you parked, and find your way back without a hitch. Your guide is Pitri, our mascot. From "where should I park?" before you stop to "where did I leave it?" after, Pitto has you covered.

How it works

Four steps, zero guesswork.

1

Enter your destination

Just type where you're headed. Pitto starts finding places to park nearby.

2

Compare nearby spots

Line up candidates and compare walking time and conditions, so you can pick with confidence.

3

Save where you parked

Once you park, save it with a single tap—add a note for the floor or section, and a photo too.

4

Head back to your car

After your errands, follow Pitri straight back to the spot you saved.
