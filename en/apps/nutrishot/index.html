NutriShot

Estimate PFC from a photo.
Review, then log your meal.

NutriShot estimates protein, fat and carbohydrates from a meal photo. Review the name and amount before saving the meal on your device.

We are preparing the app for release. Distribution links will appear here when available. The app interface is currently in Japanese.

Estimate from a photo

After your consent, a captured or selected meal photo is sent to Google Gemini for analysis. Analysis requires an internet connection.

Review before saving

Edit the meal name and adjust the amount. PFC and calories scale with the amount. Changing the name alone does not re-analyze nutrients.

Review your records

Compare today’s totals with your targets. View recorded days and averages for this week, this month or all time. Unrecorded meals and days are not included in averages.

Manage store subscriptions

An auto-renewing subscription is required. Review the price, billing period and any offer available to you in the app and the store confirmation screen before purchasing.
