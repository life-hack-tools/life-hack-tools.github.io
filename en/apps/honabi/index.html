About

Search, check,
and keep moving.

Honabi (ほなび) is an app for sole proprietors and small businesses to look for subsidies and grants in Japan. Narrow down programs from the national government, prefectures and municipalities by region, industry and what you want to do, then see on one screen whether you are likely eligible, how much you might receive and how much work the application takes. Save the programs you care about and track them with progress, notes and a reminder before the deadline.

The app is in Japanese only. It covers programs in Japan (national, prefectural and municipal).

Please note

Honabi is not an official app of any government agency. It is an unofficial app. The information in the app is collected every day from public sources such as jGrants, J-Net21 and local governments, and organized for easier reading. Always check the latest and exact conditions on the official page of each agency.

How it works

Narrow it down in 3 steps.

1

Search with your conditions

Narrow down programs by keyword, prefecture, industry, number of employees, what you want to do, deadline, and how much preparation it takes.

2

Check the details

The decision summary shows whether you are likely eligible, the amount, how to apply, what to prepare, the deadline and the effort involved. For the official terms, open the official page from the details screen.

3

Save and follow up

Save the programs you care about, keep progress and notes, and set a reminder before the deadline.

Features

The hard parts, summarized first.

Detailed filters

Filter national, prefectural and municipal programs by keyword, prefecture, industry, number of employees, what you want to do, deadline, and preparation effort.

Eligibility at a glance

Enter your business type, prefecture, number of employees, years in business and industry, and the list shows whether each program's conditions seem to match. This is only a guide.

Decision summary

Eligibility, amount, how to apply, what to prepare, deadline and effort in one place, with a link to the official page.

New, updated, closing soon

See newly listed programs, updated programs and programs closing soon in the New tab.

Save and track progress

For each saved program, record its progress (for example: interested, checking, preparing, applied) and keep notes.

Deadline reminder

Set a reminder for a saved program that notifies you before the deadline (a notification on your device).

Multiple profiles

Save several sets of conditions (profiles) and switch between them. You can use a nickname instead of a real name.

Data stays on your device

No account needed. Your profiles, saved programs, progress and notes are stored on your device.
