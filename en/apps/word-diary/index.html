FAQ

Before you start

Does it cost anything?

No. It is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Do I need an account?

No. We do not collect your name or email address.

Where are my entries stored?

On your device, and in the cloud for sync and device transfer. We do not share them with third parties. See the privacy policy for details.

What happens when I change phones?

Show a QR code on the old device and scan it with the new one to move your entries.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad (iOS 15.1 or later) and Android. The app is available in Japanese, English and Vietnamese.
