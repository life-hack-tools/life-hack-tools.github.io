Why StockHome?

When you can't see your stock,
you quietly waste money and time.

Checking the shelf before every shopping trip. Thinking you had bought it, then finding it empty. Two people in the house buying the same thing. When what you own is invisible, the small waste adds up every single day.

StockHome puts your household stock on your phone so that everyone in the house sees the same inventory. No more relying on "someone probably remembers".

How it works

Three steps. Start today.

1

Add your stock

Pick from presets or scan a barcode. Food, daily essentials, medicine — added in seconds.

2

Low stock goes on the list

When something runs low it is added to your shopping list automatically. Let it auto-consume at your usage pace, too.

3

Share with the household

Send an invite code and the whole household shares one inventory. Someone buys it, everyone sees it. No double purchases.

Features

Just what you need, done well.

Auto shopping list

When stock drops below your threshold, it goes on the shopping list by itself. Nothing forgotten.

Auto-consume

Set how fast you use something and the quantity goes down on its own. No daily data entry.

Barcode entry

Scan a product's barcode to add it. Next time, a single scan records the restock.

Expiry dates

Record an expiry date and get a reminder 3 days before, the day before, and on the day. Less food waste.

Low-stock alerts

A notification only on days there is something to buy. Your phone nudges you before you head out.

Share with family or roommates

Just send an invite code. One inventory and one shopping list, synced in real time.

PRO

Product photos

Attach a photo to each item so look-alike packages are easy to tell apart.

CSV export

Export your inventory and change history as CSV. Your data stays yours.

Multiple places

Home, parents' house, cabin, office. Keep a separate inventory for each place.

3 languages

Japanese, English, and Vietnamese. Everyone in the house, in their own language.
