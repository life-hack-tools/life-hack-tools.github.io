About

Keep rehearsal
moving.

Skip the long intro and start from where the dance begins. Repeat just the chorus. Chain a few songs into a medley for your showcase. Taali takes the friction out of rehearsal for dance teams. Search for a song by name or paste a YouTube URL. No account is needed.

* An internet connection is required. Songs play in the official YouTube player, so videos that do not allow embedded playback cannot be played.

How it works

Three steps to start.

1

Find and add a song

Search by name, or paste a YouTube URL to add it.

2

Set the section

Move the bar, listen, then tap "From here" / "To here". A zoomed bar lets you fine-tune within 10 seconds either way.

3

Play right away

Tap Play and it starts from your chosen point. Run a virtual medley of several songs, or loop.

Features

Made for the pain points of practice.

Start and end per song

Play just the section you want to practice and skip long intros.

Virtual medley

Play several songs back to back and reorder them easily. Loop one song or the whole list.

Instant cue

Tap Play and it starts right from your chosen point. Repeat from the top as often as you like.

Previous / Play / Next

Big buttons at the bottom that work even with sweaty hands. Jump back to the top of a song or on to the next one in one tap.

Tempo control

0.75x to 1.0x without changing pitch, ideal for learning choreography.

Share your setlist

Share a link via LINE or any app and teammates open it with the same settings. Share a cue sheet of songs and sections as plain text.

Taali never saves or merges audio. It only controls the playback position of the official YouTube player, so views count as usual.
