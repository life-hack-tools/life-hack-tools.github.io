Peckish

Decide tonight's meal, fast.

About

End the "what should
we eat?" loop — for fun.

Peckish makes deciding where and what to eat tonight fun. Choose how you decide based on your mood, and find tonight's meal from real restaurants near you. Turn deciding from a chore into a thrill.

Features

Three playful ways to decide.

🏆

Food Tournament

Pick winners in quick two-choice duels and land on tonight's strongest craving. Just tap your gut.

🎛️

Appetite Mixer

Blend how much, temperature, intensity, and feel across four sliders to get the restaurant that fits.

👆

Quick Pick

Glance at a row of food photos and tap the one that calls to you. See it, choose it.

📍

Real places near you

Candidates use real restaurant data nearby, so there's no "I decided but the place doesn't exist."
