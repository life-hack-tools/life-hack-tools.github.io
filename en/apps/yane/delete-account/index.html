Delete Your Account

This page explains how to delete your account in Yane: Roommate Bills & Chores, along with the data linked to it. The App is operated by Life Hack Tools.

01Delete in the App

  1. Open Yane and go to the "Members" screen (the list of members in your home).
  2. Tap "Delete account" at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Tap "Delete" to confirm.

Deletion cannot be undone.

02What Is Deleted

  • Your Yane account (your Firebase Authentication user). If you linked an Apple or Google account, that link is deleted as well.
  • Your membership in every home you belong to
  • Your name within each home. After deletion, you are shown as "Former member".
  • Settings stored on your device, and chore reminders

03What Stays in the Home

Expense, settlement and chore records stay in the home so that your housemates' balances do not change. In those records your name is shown as "Former member", and no information linked to you remains.

04If You Have Deleted the App or Cannot Open It

If you have already deleted the App or cannot open it, please use the contact form below: choose "Data deletion request" as the topic and "Yane" as the app, and write "Account deletion request". We will handle your request within 30 days of receiving it.

To identify your account, please include the email address of the Apple or Google account you linked (if you chose "Hide My Email" with Apple, the relay address).
