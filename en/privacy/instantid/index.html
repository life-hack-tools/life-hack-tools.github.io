Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 21, 2026

Life Hack Tools ("we") sets out the following privacy policy regarding how user information is handled in InstantID (an ID photo maker; the "App"). The App requires no account and can be used anonymously. All photo processing happens on your device — your photos are never uploaded to any external server.

01Information We Handle

The App handles the following information.

  • Photos: Photos you capture with the camera or select from your photo library are used to create ID photos. All processing happens on your device, and photos are never sent to an external server. The ID photos you create are saved to your device (e.g. camera roll) by your own action.
  • Crash logs & diagnostics: Collected via Sentry to improve the App's stability and quality (analytics purposes).
  • Advertising identifier: In the free version, Google AdMob may use an advertising identifier (such as IDFA) to serve ads.
  • Purchase information: To manage the Pro (one-time) purchase, we use the purchase status processed by in-app purchase (Apple). We never hold payment details such as credit card numbers.

The App does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address or phone number.

02Purpose of Use

  • Creating, saving, sharing and generating print sheets of ID photos
  • Improving app quality and fixing issues
  • Displaying ads (free version)
  • Managing and restoring the Pro (one-time) purchase

03Third-Party Services

The App uses the following external services to provide its features. Each service handles data in accordance with its own privacy policy.

  • Google AdMob (Google): Uses advertising identifiers and similar to serve ads in the free version.
  • Sentry: Used to collect diagnostic information for crash analysis and quality improvement (analytics purposes).
  • In-app purchase (Apple): Used to process and manage the Pro (one-time) purchase.

These are used only to the extent necessary to provide the App's features. We never sell user information to third parties.

04Permissions

The App uses the following device permissions to provide its features.

  • Camera: To take your ID photo.
  • Photo library: To choose a photo to turn into an ID photo, and to save the photo you create.

05Advertising & App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

On iOS, you can choose whether to allow tracking under App Tracking Transparency (ATT). You can still use the App if you decline, though the ads shown may be less relevant to your interests.

06Data Storage & Deletion

The ID photos you create are stored on your device. You can delete them from your camera roll, or delete all data by uninstalling the App from your device. If you wish to have any other data deleted, please contact us at the address below.

07Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

08Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as needed. Any changes will be published on this page.
