Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Life Hack Tools ("we") handles user information in Pitto (a parking notes & navigation app; "the App"). The App requires no account and can be used anonymously. We never share the information we collect with third parties for advertising purposes, nor do we sell it. Data is encrypted in transit.

01Information We Collect & Use

The App may handle the following information:

  • Location data (precise current location): Used to search and compare parking near your destination, and to show walking routes to your destination and to your parked spot. Location data is sent to Google Maps Platform (Places API, Routes API, Maps SDK) for processing.
  • Parked spot records: We save the coordinates of your parked spot, notes such as floor or section, and photos.
  • Photos: You can take a photo with the camera as a landmark for your parked spot (optional).
  • Crash logs: Collected via Sentry to improve app quality (analytics purposes).
  • Anonymous identifier: An anonymous identifier via Supabase is used to sync across devices without an account. Related data is stored on Supabase servers.

The App does not collect personally identifiable information such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers.

02How We Use Information

  • Searching and comparing parking near your destination
  • Showing walking routes to your destination and parked spot
  • Recording your parked spot (coordinates, notes, photos) and syncing across devices
  • Improving app quality and fixing issues

03Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services to provide its features. Each service handles data according to its own privacy policy.

  • Google Maps Platform (Google LLC / Places API, Routes API, Maps SDK): Receives your location and search criteria to search and compare parking and to display walking routes.
  • Supabase: Used for cross-device sync and data storage via an account-free anonymous identifier.
  • Sentry: Used to collect diagnostic data for crash analysis and quality improvement (analytics purposes).

These services are used only to the extent necessary to provide the App's features. We do not sell user information to third parties.

04Permissions

The App uses the following device permissions to provide its features:

  • Location: To search and compare nearby parking and display walking routes.
  • Camera: To take a photo as a landmark for your parked spot (optional).

05Data Storage & Deletion

Your parked spot records and settings are stored in the app and in the sync service (Supabase). You can delete data by removing records within the app or by uninstalling the app from your device. To request deletion of any other data, please contact us below.

06Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

07Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as needed. Changes will be posted on this page.
