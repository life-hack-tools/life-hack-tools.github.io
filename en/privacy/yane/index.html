Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Life Hack Tools ("we") handles user information in Yane: Roommate Bills & Chores ("the App"). The App lets people who live together — roommates, couples and shared houses — share their records of money and chores and keep in touch with each other. No account is required. Only members of the same home can view and edit that home's data, and we never sell or provide user information to third parties. The App shows no ads.

01Account and Identifier

The App does not require you to create an account. When the App starts, it creates an identifier for each device using anonymous sign-in with Firebase Authentication, a service provided by Google. This identifier is used to determine which home you are a member of.

We never ask you to enter an email address, phone number or name. Linking an Apple or Google account is optional (see the next section).

02Linking an Account (Optional)

You can use the App without signing up. If you wish, you can link an Apple or Google account (Apple and Google on iPhone; Google on Android). Once linked, you can get back to the same homes and records after changing phones or reinstalling the App.

When you link an account, we receive the following information and store it in Firebase Authentication:

  • The user identifier issued by Apple or Google
  • The email address provided by Apple or Google (if you choose "Hide My Email" with Apple, a relay address)

Your email address is never shown to your housemates, and we do not use it to contact you or for advertising.

03Information We Store

To share it within your home, the App stores the following information that you enter:

  • Home and housemates: the home's name and currency, and each housemate's display name and color. A display name is whatever you choose to call someone; it does not have to be a real name.
  • Money records: expenses paid on behalf of others (title, amount, date, who paid, and how it is split), their change history, and settlement records.
  • Chore rotations: the name, interval and order of each chore, and records of completion.
  • Shared supplies: the stock of shared household supplies and the shopping list.
  • Comments and House notes: comments on records (expenses, chores, supplies and settlements) and posts on "House notes", the board that all members of the home can see, including who posted them and when (section 05).
  • Photos: photos attached to comments or notes in House notes (section 04).
  • "Ask Yane": the announcements sent and a record of who requested them (section 06).
  • Reports: reports you send (section 07).
  • Notification registrations: the registration of your device for push notifications (section 08).
  • Invitations and joining: invite codes and requests to join a home (including requests to move a housemate's slot to a new device when changing phones).

04Photos

You can attach photos you choose or take to comments and notes in House notes. The App uses your photo library or camera only when you choose to attach a photo.

  • Before a photo is sent, it is resized on your device to a JPEG whose longer side is 1600 pixels. At that point, EXIF data, including location information such as where the photo was taken, is removed.
  • The resized photo is stored in Firebase Storage, a service provided by Google, in the Tokyo region.
  • Photos can be seen only by current members of that home.
  • If you delete a post that has a photo, the photo is kept as part of the home's records (it is no longer shown in the comment or note). Photos are deleted together with the home when the home is dissolved (when its last member deletes their account).

05Comments and House Notes

In the App, you can post on "House notes", a message board that all members of the home can see, and comment on records (expenses, chores, supplies and settlements). The text is stored in Cloud Firestore.

  • When you delete a post, its text is removed and a line saying it was deleted remains in its place.
  • Posts by people who have left the home or deleted their account remain as part of the home's records. They are deleted together with the home when it is dissolved.

06"Ask Yane"

"Ask Yane" sends a prepared message to all members of the home as an announcement from Yane, without showing the name of the person who requested it. Members of the home cannot see who requested it.

However, to investigate misuse such as harassment, we keep a record of who requested each announcement that only the operator can see.

07Reports

When you report a comment or a note in House notes, the following information is sent to the operator. These notices are sent to a restricted channel in Slack, the tool we use for internal communication.

  • The reason for the report
  • The reported post (its text, and whether it has photos)
  • The in-home IDs of the person who posted it and the person who reported it
  • The home's ID

Housemates' display names and the home's name are not included in these notices. The operator may review the reported post and its photos to the extent necessary to handle the report.

08Notifications

If you allow notifications, we send push notifications when a member of your home posts in House notes or writes a comment, and when there is an "Ask Yane" announcement. To do this, we store the following for each device in Cloud Firestore. Only you and our server that sends notifications can read it.

  • Your device's notification token
  • Your display language (used for the notification text)
  • Your settings for which types of notifications to receive
  • The IDs of housemates you have hidden on that device (we do not send you notifications from them)

Notifications are relayed through Expo's notification service and delivered to your device by Apple's or Google's notification service. The notification text includes the home's name, the poster's display name and part of the post. You can turn off notifications at any time in your device settings.

Chore reminders remain local notifications scheduled only on your device.

09Crash Reporting

To find and fix problems, the App sends error information to Sentry. This includes the details of the error, the screen or process where it occurred, your device model, and your OS and app versions. To check how quickly the App runs, it may also send how long some operations took.

The App is configured not to send what you enter, such as expense titles, housemates' display names or comments. Sentry stores this data in the United States.

10Where Data Is Stored and External Services

Home data is stored in Cloud Firestore and photos in Firebase Storage. Both are services provided by Google; the data is stored in the Tokyo region (asia-northeast1), in a project used only for the App. Sending notifications, receiving reports, "Ask Yane" and deleting dissolved homes are handled by our server on Google Cloud Run.

The App uses services from the following companies, which handle information on our behalf only to the extent necessary to provide those services:

  • Google: Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Storage), Cloud Run, Sign in with Google, and Android's notification service
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple and iPhone's notification service
  • Expo: relaying push notifications
  • Sentry: error reporting
  • Slack: notifying the operator of reports

11Who Can See Your Information

Within the App, a home's data (including comments, notes in House notes and photos) can be viewed and edited only by current members of that home. This restriction is enforced by Cloud Firestore and Firebase Storage security rules. Members who leave a home can no longer see its data.

We never sell or provide user information to third parties. The App shows no ads.

12What We Do Not Use

The App does not use:

  • Analytics tools
  • Advertising SDKs

The App also does not use your location, contacts or microphone. It uses your photo library and camera only when you attach a photo (section 04).

13Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following on your device:

  • Your display language setting
  • Your sign-in state (anonymous sign-in, or sign-in with a linked account)
  • Your settings for which types of notifications to receive, and the IDs of housemates you have hidden
  • Scheduled chore reminders

14Deleting Your Account and Data

You can delete your account by tapping "Delete account" at the bottom of the "Members" screen (the list of members in your home) and then tapping "Delete" to confirm. Whichever homes you belong to, the following is deleted:

  • Your account in the App (your Firebase Authentication user). If you linked an Apple or Google account, that link is deleted as well. If you linked an Apple account, we also revoke the App's Sign in with Apple authorization on Apple's side.
  • Your membership in every home you belong to
  • Settings stored on your device, and chore reminders

The data of each home you belong to is handled as follows:

  • A home that other housemates have joined: You leave the home, your name within it is removed, and you are shown as "Former member". Expense, settlement and chore records stay in the home so that your housemates' balances do not change. Comments and notes in House notes you wrote, and photos you attached, also stay in the home. In those records your name is also shown as "Former member", and no information linked to you remains.
  • A home that only you have joined: The home is deleted along with all of its records, comments, notes in House notes and photos. Housemates added by name only, who have not installed the App, are not counted as having joined. The deletion is carried out on our server and is usually completed within about an hour. The home's invite codes stop working as soon as you delete your account, so housemates added by name only can no longer join later.

If you have already deleted the App or cannot open it, please send an "Account deletion request" via the contact form below. We will handle it within 30 days of receiving it. To identify your account, please include the email address of the Apple or Google account you linked.

For details, see Delete Your Account.

15In-App Purchases

There are currently no purchases in the App.

We plan to introduce a home plan (subscription) in the future. Payments will be processed by Apple or Google, and we will not receive your credit card information. We will update this policy when the plan is introduced.

16Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for anyone under the age of 18.

We may update this policy from time to time. If we do, we will announce the changes on this page.
