Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This policy explains how Life Hack Tools (“we”) handles your information in Taali: Dance Practice Player (“the app”). The app needs no account, collects no personal information, and contains no ad SDK, tracking, or analytics SDK.

01Our approach

The app has no login or account. We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or location. The app contains no advertising mechanism, no tracking of user behavior, and no analytics SDK.

02Information stored on your device

The following information is stored only on your device and is never sent elsewhere.

  • Your setlist (added songs, start and end of each song, and order)
  • Settings (display language, tempo, loop, and so on)

This information is deleted when you uninstall the app.

03Share links and cue sheets

Only when you choose to share, the app creates a share link (URL) or a plain-text list of songs and sections (a cue sheet) and hands it to your device's share sheet. A share link contains the song IDs and section information. You choose the destination in the share sheet; the app never sends anything automatically. Links and text you send to other apps or services, such as LINE, are outside our control.

04Song search

When you search for a song, your search terms are sent through our API (Cloudflare Workers) to the YouTube Data API (Google). Search terms are not linked to any information that identifies you and are not stored on our server. The results of the same search are cached for a short time. To limit access and prevent excessive use, we may temporarily process IP addresses.

05YouTube embedded playback and ads

Videos are played in the official YouTube embedded player (YouTube IFrame Player). For this reason, YouTube may show ads and collect viewing data on its own. This is done under YouTube’s terms and policies, and we are not involved. The app itself shows no ads.

The app does not download, save, convert, or export audio or video. It only controls the playback position.

Your use of YouTube in the app is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service. For how Google handles data, see the Google Privacy Policy.

06Update checks

To check whether an app update is available, we send technical information such as a randomly generated install ID, the app version, and the OS type to Expo (EAS Update, 650 Industries, Inc.). This does not include your setlists or search terms.

07Diagnostics and analytics

The app does not include crash reporting, usage analytics, or any mechanism for advertising.

08Permissions

The app does not use the camera, microphone, photos, location, or contacts permissions.

09Children

The app is not directed at children and is intended for people aged 18 and over.

10Deleting your data

You can delete your setlist in the app at any time. Uninstalling the app deletes everything stored on your device. We hold no setlists on our servers and do not store search terms, so you do not need to send us a deletion request.

11Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as needed. Any changes will be posted on this page.

For questions about this policy, please contact us using the form below.
