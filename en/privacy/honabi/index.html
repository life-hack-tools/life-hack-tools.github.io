Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 28, 2026

This policy explains how Life Hack Tools (“we”) handles your information in Honabi, an app for finding subsidies and grants in Japan (“the app”). This policy covers the app distributed on the App Store and Google Play (iPhone and Android). The app needs no account. The app itself is in Japanese only.

01Our approach

The app does not send the profiles you enter, the programs you save, your progress or your notes to our servers. We do not receive or store any of this.

02Information stored on your device

The following is stored only on your device:

  • Profiles used to show whether programs may fit you (prefecture, business type, number of employees, years in business, industry, what you want to do), and the name you give each profile (a nickname is fine)
  • Saved programs
  • Progress and notes for each program
  • The scheduled time of deadline reminders

Search keywords and filter conditions are used on your device to narrow the list and are not sent. All of this is deleted when you delete the app.

03Loading subsidy and grant data

The app loads subsidy and grant information from data we publish (honabi-data.pages.dev, served by Cloudflare Pages of Cloudflare, Inc.). It only loads public data that anyone can view. Your profiles, saved programs, notes and search keywords are not sent.

As with visiting any website, technical information needed for the connection (such as your IP address and the name of the data file loaded) reaches the server. When you open a program's details, the app loads the detail data for that program.

04Notifications (deadline reminders)

When you turn on a deadline reminder for a saved program, the app notifies you at 9:00 (Japan time) three days before the deadline, using a notification scheduled on your device (local notification). It does not go through a server, and the app does not obtain a push notification token.

The app asks for notification permission the first time you turn on a reminder. The rest of the app works without it. You can change the permission at any time in your device settings.

05Checking for app updates

To check whether an app update is available, the app sends technical information such as a randomly generated installation ID, the app version and the OS type to Expo (EAS Update, 650 Industries, Inc.). Your profiles, saved programs and notes are not included.

06Diagnostics

When the app crashes, or when it cannot load the subsidy and grant data, it sends the error details, the app version, the OS type and version, and the device model to the error monitoring service Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) so we can find the cause. Information that identifies you, such as your name or email address, is not attached.

The current version contains no usage analytics and no advertising.

07External web pages

When you open an official page or application page from a program's details, the agency's website opens in your device's browser or similar. How that site handles information is governed by that site's own policy.

08Permissions

The only device permission the app asks for is notifications. It does not use location, camera, photos, contacts or the microphone.

09Deleting your data

You can delete the following in the app:

  • Saved programs: tap “★ 保存済み” (Saved) on a program's details to unsave it (its reminder is also turned off)
  • Progress and notes: “進み具合とメモを消す” (Clear progress and notes) in the Saved tab
  • Deadline reminders: tap an active reminder in the Saved tab to turn it off
  • Profiles: when you have two or more, “選択中のプロフィールを削除” (Delete selected profile)

Deleting the app removes all information stored on your device. Because we have no profiles, saved programs or notes of yours on our servers, there is no need to ask us to delete anything.

10Changes to this policy

We may change this policy when needed. Any changes will be published on this page.
