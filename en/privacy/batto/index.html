Privacy Policy

Effective: September 11, 2026 / Last updated: September 20, 2026

Life Hack Tools ("we") sets out the following privacy policy regarding how user information is handled in Batto (a baseball scorebook app; the "App"). The App requires no account registration and no login, and can be used anonymously. Your game records are stored only on your device, and their contents leave your device only when you choose to share a score. However, if you choose to export a game, the App turns the game record into a CSV or JSON file and hands it to the destination you pick (a place to save files, or another app). That file is never sent to our servers. Separately, we collect crash logs and performance data to detect and fix problems in the App and to improve how it runs. If you send feedback from the "Settings & support" screen, we also receive what you send, together with the app and device information we need to look into it. The App shows no advertising and performs no tracking for advertising purposes.

01Information Stored Only on Your Device

The following information is stored only in your device's local storage. It is never sent anywhere automatically.

  • In-progress game data: The contents of the game you are recording (innings, runs, at-bat results and so on) are stored on your device (storage key: batto.activeGame.v1).
  • Team information: Team names, batting orders, player names and uniform numbers are stored on your device (storage key: batto.teamProfiles.v1).
  • Past games: The games you have recorded (team names, runs, at-bat results and so on) are stored on your device, up to 100 games. You can delete them one at a time from the "Past games" screen.
  • Record of scores you have shared: For each score you share, the URL, the title (team names and score), when it was shared, and the key used to stop sharing it are stored on your device, up to 50 entries.

This data exists only on your device and is removed when you uninstall the App.

However, if you choose to export a game from the "Past games" screen, the App turns that game's record (including player names) into a CSV or JSON file and hands it to the destination you pick (a place to save files, or another app). That file is never sent to our servers.

02Information Sent When You Share a Score

The game data and team information you enter is sent externally only when you choose to share a score. When you do, the following information is sent to Supabase's servers, as needed to provide the sharing feature.

  • Both team names
  • Both teams' scores
  • The inning and the state of the game
  • The game title
  • The batting orders (the list of player names for both teams) — depending on what you enter, these may be real names
  • The 12 most recent at-bat log entries (display text that includes player names, such as "Top 1st — Kenta Sato: single to left … 1 run!")
  • Structured at-bat event data (inning, batting order position, result of the at-bat, and runner advances)

That is the full extent of what is sent. No photos, location data or contacts are ever transmitted. All communication is encrypted (HTTPS / TLS).

Once shared, this information remains stored on Supabase's servers until you stop sharing it.

03Who Can See a Shared Link, and Deletion

When you share a score, a viewing URL is issued. That URL contains a hard-to-guess ID, but anyone who knows the URL can view the shared score. The App does not provide any mechanism to restrict who can view it (such as a password or invitation).

Because shared content includes player names, please be careful about who you send the URL to.

You can stop sharing a score at any time from the "Shared scores" screen in the app. Once you do, the link no longer shows anything.

The key used to take a score down is stored only on the device that shared it. If that device's copy is lost — for example when you change phones or delete the app — you can no longer take it down from the app. Please use the contact form on our support page instead.

04Crash Logs and Performance Monitoring

The App collects the following diagnostic information via Sentry in order to detect and fix problems in the App and to improve how it runs (quality improvement / analytics purposes).

  • Crash and error information: Error information at the time of a crash or error, your device model, OS version, app version and similar diagnostic information that Sentry collects automatically.
  • Performance data: Measurements of how fast the App runs, such as how long it takes to start up and to complete network requests, and whether screen rendering stalls. Not every measurement is sent; about 20% are selected at random.

We use Sentry with settings that keep personally identifiable information from being sent. We also do not implement any deliberate transmission of the game data or player names you enter. We never sell the diagnostic information we collect to third parties or provide it for advertising purposes.

05Information Sent When You Send Feedback

From the App's "Settings & support" screen, you can send us feedback, bug reports, questions and data deletion requests. The following information is sent only when you tap the send button.

  • What you write: The type (feedback / bug / question / data deletion request) and your message
  • Reply email address (optional): Sent only if you enter one. It is used only to reply to you.
  • App and device information: App name; app version, build number and runtimeVersion; OS (iOS / Android) and OS version; your device's language setting; and your device model

The app and device information is attached automatically so that we can investigate problems. Your game data, team information and shared scores are never attached automatically (unless you write them in your message).

What you send passes through our intake service on Cloudflare (form.lh.tools) and arrives in a private channel in our Slack workspace. On receipt, the time received, the country it was sent from (determined by Cloudflare from the IP address) and a Cloudflare request ID are added.

The sender's IP address is used to limit how many messages can be sent in a short period. It is not included in what reaches Slack, and we do not store it.

We use what you send only to respond to you and to investigate and improve the App, and we keep it for as long as needed to handle your request. We never sell it to third parties or provide it for advertising purposes. For how the contact form on our website handles information, see the Life Hack Tools privacy policy.

06Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect any of the following.

  • Personally identifiable information such as your name or phone number (we receive an email address only if you enter one yourself as the reply address for feedback)
  • Photos or camera imagery
  • Location data
  • Contacts (address book)

The App also shows no advertising and performs no tracking for advertising purposes. We never sell or share user information with third parties.

07Third-Party Services

The App uses the following external services to provide its features. Each service handles data in accordance with its own privacy policy.

  • Supabase: Used solely for the score sharing feature (storing shared data and issuing the viewing URL). It is not used for data synchronisation or backup.
  • Sentry: Used to collect diagnostic information for crash analysis and performance monitoring (quality improvement / analytics purposes).
  • Cloudflare: Used to run our feedback intake service (form.lh.tools).
  • Slack: Used to receive submitted feedback in our private channel.

Unless you choose to share a score, your game data and team information are never sent externally (if you choose to export a game, the file only goes to the destination you pick and is not sent to our servers).

08Permissions

The App does not require sensitive device permissions such as camera, photo library, location or contacts.

09Data Storage & Deletion

  • On-device data (games and team information): Deleted when you uninstall the App from your device.
  • Shared scores: Stored on Supabase's servers until you stop sharing them. When you stop sharing from the "Shared scores" screen in the App, they are deleted from the server. If you can no longer do so because this device's copy has been lost, please contact us through the form below.
  • Crash logs and performance data: Stored on Sentry's service.
  • Feedback: Stored in a private channel in our Slack workspace and kept for as long as needed to handle your request.

10Children's Privacy

The App is intended for people aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as needed. Any changes will be published on this page.

12Contact

For inquiries regarding this policy, or to request deletion of a score you have shared, please contact us below. You can also send a message from the App's "Settings & support" screen.
