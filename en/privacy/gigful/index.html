Gigful Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026.

Life Hack Tools handles information in Gigful, including its event storage, sharing, public pages and purchase features, as described below.

Event data

Event data is processed on your device and with Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Cloud Functions). Event names, dates, venues, ticket types, costs, revenue sharing, assignments, teams, borrowed items and schedules are stored and synchronized. Free-text fields may contain other people’s names or contact details. Enter only what you need.

Some fields, such as staff rosters and individual tickets, are device-only. However, event information and flyer images selected for a public page are separately sent to the server when you publish.

Accounts

Signing in with an external account is optional. Choose 「あとで」 (Later) on the initial screen to continue. When server features are used, Firebase Authentication issues an anonymous user ID to manage event ownership and access. Anonymous use does not mean that data stays off the server.

On iOS, you may sign in with Apple to link the anonymous account and regain access after changing devices. Firebase records an email address, including an Apple relay address if you hide your email. The app does not request your name from Apple.

Sharing

When you create an invitation link, participants can view or edit the event according to their permissions. The server stores owner and participant IDs, permissions, invitation IDs and expiry information, records of removed participants and update times. Do not post private invitation links publicly.

Venue search and optional location

When you focus the venue input, the app may request location permission while using the app to suggest nearby venues. If allowed, device latitude and longitude are sent with your search text through Cloud Functions and the LHTz places gateway to Google Places API for nearby suggestions and distances. You can still search without location if you deny permission or location is unavailable. The app does not track location in the background.

Selecting a result sends its place ID through the same route and returns its address, phone number, map URL and venue coordinates. Venue coordinates are different from your current location. The venue name is stored as part of the event. The app does not add your user ID or email to the Google Places search request. Maps use Apple Maps on iOS and Google’s map service on Android.

Public event pages and flyer images

Publishing from 「宣伝」 (Promotion) sends your selected title, date, venue, promotional text and other public details to the server. People with the link can view them at https://gigful.lh.tools/e/… .

Selected flyer images are first copied to your device. Publishing uploads the first flyer image to the server for the public page; images do not always stay on the device. Check that images and public text do not expose personal information. Unpublishing cannot remove copies already saved by viewers.

Performer recruitment

When an organizer publishes recruitment, the title, date, venue, conditions, contact details and performance duration appear at https://gigful.lh.tools/r/… . Applicants can submit their name, contact details, act, duration and message without signing in. Submissions are stored in Firebase and made available to the organizer in the app. Connection information such as the applicant’s IP address is processed to handle requests and limit repeated abusive submissions. Applicants may request deletion through the organizer or the contact below.

Crash reports with consent

Sentry crash reporting is off by default. It is enabled only when you turn on 「クラッシュ情報を送る」 in Settings, and you can turn it off later. Reports are limited to app version, OS, device class, error location and similar technical details. The app removes error text, interaction history, event content, user IDs and IP addresses from the report it constructs. The receiving service still receives connection information such as your IP address.

In-app purchases

Gigful Pro is a one-time in-app purchase paid through Apple or Google Play. RevenueCat processes purchase records and its anonymous identifier to verify purchases, obtain access rights and restore them. The app does not set and send your name, email or event content to RevenueCat. Automatic device identifier collection is disabled in the app’s configuration, and advertising IDs are not used for measurement. Purchase records are also handled under the policies of the store you use.

Updates and diagnostics

The app checks Expo’s update service at launch. The service processes OS, project and runtime information, a random per-installation identifier (EAS-Client-ID), connection IP address and related information. If a previous update launch recorded a fatal error, part of it (up to 1,024 characters) may be sent with a later update request. This is separate from the Sentry consent setting. Event text is not automatically attached to update requests.

Photos, camera, advertising and tracking

The app handles flyer and ticket-design images you choose. Public flyer uploads work as described above. The camera reads admission-ticket QR codes; camera images and video are not stored or sent. The app does not access your address book, microphone or advertising ID. It includes no advertising SDK and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Exported files

When you export and share files such as PDFs, you choose the destination using the operating system. The destination service’s policies also apply. Deletion by the operator does not automatically delete exported files, OS backups or copies saved by other people.

Storage and deletion requests

Server data is kept until you request deletion. The operator may delete shared events that have not been updated for more than two years. Removing an event from the device list is different from deleting its server data or account. The account screen currently has no operation to delete a server-side account.

Request deletion at info@lh.tools and include information that identifies the target, such as the event name and date. We use the account ID to verify the requester and handle the request within 30 days. Include the account ID if you know it; contact us if you do not know how to find it. If you use Sign in with Apple, also provide that email address. Expired invitations and records of removed participants are not deleted automatically; include them in your request if you want them deleted. Store purchase records and external copies may not be directly deletable by the operator.

Support and service providers

Sending an in-app support request sends its text, an optional reply email and diagnostic information such as OS, device model and app version to form.lh.tools for support. The entire event is not automatically attached.

These features use Firebase, Google Places/maps, Apple, Sentry, RevenueCat, Expo and the support intake service. Providers receive connection information such as IP addresses when processing requests. Information you invite others to access, publish or share externally is also visible to those recipients or public-page viewers.
