Gigful Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026.

Life Hack Tools (the "Operator") handles information in Gigful as follows.

Event data

In the released app, event data is saved by default in Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Cloud Functions, which run in the Tokyo region). What is saved: event name, date, venue, capacity, staff count, ticket types, expenses, splits, people in charge, teams and set times, borrowed items, and the schedule. Names of people in charge and lenders are free text that you type and may include other people's names. Enter only what you need.

The staff roster, vendors and contacts, food and drink orders, sponsors, individual tickets (including buyer names), expected attendance, copies of recruitment links, venue details, and promotion materials stay on your device only and are not sent to the server.

Account

No email address or sign-up is required. When you open the app, Firebase Authentication issues an anonymous user ID. This ID marks who owns an event on the server.

Optionally, you can sign in with Apple (iOS) to restore your events after changing devices or reinstalling. Your anonymous account is linked to your Apple account, and Firebase records an email address (Apple's relay address if you choose to hide your email). We do not ask for your name and do not store it.

Sharing

When you create an invitation link, anyone who opens it can view or edit that event with the permission you set (edit or view). The server stores the user IDs and permissions of the creator and members, invitation details (ID, permission, issue time, and a 7-day expiry), a record of removed members, and update times. Do not post invitation links in public places.

Venue search

When you search for a venue, the text you type is sent through Cloud Functions to LHTz's places gateway, which queries the Google Places API. When you pick a result, its place ID is sent the same way, and the address, phone number, map URL, and coordinates that come back are saved on your device as the venue details of that event. We do not read your device's location, and we do not send your user ID or email to Google. The venue name is also saved on the server as part of the event. The small map uses Apple Maps on iOS and Google's map service on Android.

Performer recruitment

An organizer can create a public recruitment page (https://gigful-lht.web.app/r/...). It shows the title, date, venue, conditions, contact, and set length that the organizer entered. Anyone with the page can apply without signing in. The name, contact, act, set length, and message an applicant enters are saved in Firebase and can be read only by the organizer in the app. Firebase receives applicants' connection information (such as IP addresses) in order to handle the request. Applicants can ask for deletion through the organizer or the contact below.

Crash reports (only if you agree)

On first use, the app asks whether to send crash reports. Only if you agree, it sends Sentry the app version, OS name and version, device class, and where the error happened (function, file name, and line number). It does not send error messages, your activity, event content, user ID, or IP address. You can change your choice later.

In-app purchases

In versions that offer the one-time Gigful Pro purchase, Apple or Google processes the payment, and a purchase record (receipt or purchase token) and an anonymous identifier assigned by RevenueCat (United States) are sent to RevenueCat to confirm the purchase. Your name, email address, and event content are not sent. We do not use advertising IDs.

App updates

The app checks Expo's update service when it starts. The request includes the OS, project ID, and runtime, plus a random identifier per installation (EAS-Client-ID) and the connecting IP address. This identifier is not a hardware ID or an advertising ID. If a fatal error was recorded during the last update launch, part of that error (up to 1024 characters) is also sent with the next update request. Event content is not sent.

No ads, no tracking, data we do not collect

The app shows no ads and includes no advertising SDK. It does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. It does not collect your location, contacts, microphone, or advertising ID. Photos you pick as flyers in “Promotion” are copied into the app’s own storage and stay on your device; they are not uploaded. The camera is used only to read ticket QR codes in “QR check-in”; no video or image is stored or sent.

Backups and exports

JSON contains the event information you entered, and PDF contains what is in the run sheet. They are created only when you export, and you choose where to save or send them in the OS share screen. Exported files and OS backups are not deleted automatically when you delete data in the app.

Retention and deletion

Events on your device are kept until you delete them in the app or delete the app. "Remove from this device's list" deletes only the copy on your device; the data on the server remains.

Data stored on the server (shared events and your account) is kept until you ask us to delete it. The Operator may delete shared events that have not been updated for more than 2 years.

The app currently has no feature to delete events or your account from the server. To request deletion, email info@lh.tools with the name and date of the event you want deleted and, if you signed in with Apple, the email address of that account. After verifying the request, we will delete the server data within 30 days.

Expired invitations and records of members removed from a shared event are not deleted automatically. If you want them deleted, include them in the request above.

Service providers

The Operator uses or entrusts processing to Google (Firebase, Places API, and Google's map service), Apple, Sentry, RevenueCat, Expo (EAS Update), and the support intake (form.lh.tools), within the purposes above. We do not provide your data to other third parties except as required by law.

Contact

With in-app contact, we receive your message, an optional reply email address, and your device OS and model, app version and build, and language, via form.lh.tools, and use them to respond. Do not send backups that contain real names or participant information unless needed.

Contact: Life Hack Tools / Contact form / info@lh.tools
