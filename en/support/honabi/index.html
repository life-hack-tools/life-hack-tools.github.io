Support

Help and support for Honabi, an app for finding subsidies and grants in Japan

FAQ

01What language is the app in?

The app is in Japanese only. It covers programs of the national government, prefectures and municipalities in Japan.

02Is Honabi an official app?

No. Honabi is not made by any government agency. It is an unofficial app. It shows information collected every day from public sources such as jGrants, J-Net21 and local governments, organized for easier reading.

Always check the latest and exact conditions by opening the official page from a program's details. You cannot apply inside the app. Please apply through each agency's application page or office.

03I see “制度を取得できませんでした” (could not load programs)

This appears when the app cannot load the subsidy and grant data. Check your connection, then reload or reopen the tab.

04I can't find programs, or only a few

Try loosening your filters (what you want to do, deadline, preparation effort, and so on).

Your profile (eligibility conditions) is used only to show whether each program in the list seems to fit. Entering a profile does not change the number of programs in the list.

05How should I read the eligibility check?

When your profile has a prefecture, business type, number of employees, years in business and industry, the app shows whether each program's conditions seem to match. This is a guide based on public information. Check the official guidelines to confirm whether you actually qualify.

06I want to use or delete multiple profiles

In the profile area of the Search tab, you can save several sets of conditions and switch between them. A nickname such as “Restaurant A” is fine.

When you have two or more profiles, “選択中のプロフィールを削除” (Delete selected profile) deletes the one you have selected.

07How do deadline reminders work?

In the Saved tab, tap “通知をオンにする” (Turn on notification) for a saved program. The app notifies you on your device at 9:00 (Japan time) three days before the deadline. Tap the scheduled time shown while it is on to turn it off.

To use reminders, allow notifications for Honabi in your device settings. Reminders cannot be set for programs whose deadline is three days away or less, or whose deadline is unknown.

08A saved program shows “掲載終了” (no longer listed)

Programs that are no longer listed stay in your saved list. Check the official page to see whether there will be another round. To unsave a program, tap “★ 保存済み” (Saved) on its details.

09Backups and switching phones

Honabi has no accounts, and data is stored only on your device. If you delete the app or switch phones, your profiles, saved programs, progress and notes are not carried over.

10I want to delete my stored data

You can delete saved programs, progress and notes, reminders and profiles inside the app. Deleting the app removes all information stored on your device. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Contact

If the answers above do not help, please contact us using the form below. For bug reports, it helps to include your device (iPhone / Android) and what you were doing when it happened. For privacy questions, please read the Privacy Policy first and then use the same form. We cannot answer questions about the conditions of, or how to apply for, individual subsidies and grants. Please contact the agency that runs each program.
